Two Thirds of the Vertices of a Minimal Counterexample to the Erdős–Gyárfás Conjecture are Cubic

Andrew Bisch

July 2026

PDF Lean verification Zenodo DOI

Abstract

A minimal counterexample to the Erdős–Gyárfás conjecture is a graph of minimum possible order and, subject to that, minimum possible size, with minimum degree at least 3 and containing no cycle whose length is a power of 2. Carr recently showed that at least 4/7 of the vertices of any minimal counterexample have degree exactly 3. We observe that combining two structural facts already established in that work yields the stronger bound 2/3, and we prove an additional structural restriction: whenever the four edges leaving a pair of adjacent cubic vertices all end in vertices of degree at least 4, the pair has a unique common neighbor, and that common neighbor has degree exactly 4.

Keywords

Erdős–Gyárfás conjecture · cycles of power-of-two length · cubic graphs · minimal counterexamples · extremal graph theory
